About Us

Matcha, seriously sourced.

Just a girl who loves Matcha and wants to share it with th world 🍵

Our matcha and hojicha are sourced from the Japanese Yabukita Cultivar green tea tree — the gold standard for flavour, colour and nutritional quality. We also stock Ube (Purple Yam), Taro, and Butterfly Pea Flower powders for your drinks, bakes, and creative recipes.

Whether you're new to matcha or a day one matcha lover, we have the grade, the tools, and the bundles for you — delivered across Nairobi and beyond.
